## Future Trends in Encryption & Privacy Tech

What’s next? Expect more AI-driven privacy tools—but not the kind that tracks you. Think AI that *protects* you. For example, AI that detects unusual login patterns or flags suspicious file access in real time.

Zero-knowledge proofs are also going mainstream. These let you prove something is true without revealing the data. Imagine verifying a user’s identity without storing their password. That’s the future.
