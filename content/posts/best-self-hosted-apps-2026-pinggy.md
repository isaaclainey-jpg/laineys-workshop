## Why Choose Self-Hosted Apps Over Cloud Solutions

One of the biggest advantages of self-hosted apps is the level of control they provide. Unlike cloud-based services, where your data is stored on someone else’s servers, self-hosted apps let you manage everything from the hardware to the software. This is especially appealing to those who value privacy and security above all else.

Another benefit is the flexibility to customize your setup. With cloud solutions, you’re often limited by the provider’s offerings. Self-hosted apps, on the other hand, allow you to tailor your environment to fit your specific needs. This can be a game-changer for developers and power users who want full control over their tools.

Cost is also a consideration. While initial setup can be more expensive, self-hosted apps can be more cost-effective in the long run. Many open-source solutions are available at no cost, and you can scale your infrastructure as needed without paying for premium features.
