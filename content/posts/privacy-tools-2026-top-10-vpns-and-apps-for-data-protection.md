## The 10 Best Privacy Apps for 2026

Alongside the best **Privacy Tools 2026: 10 VPNs & Apps That Actually Work**, there are other apps that can help you protect your data and online identity.

1. **Signal** – For secure messaging, Signal is unmatched. It uses end-to-end encryption and has no ads or data collection.

2. **ProtonMail** – ProtonMail offers encrypted email that’s completely private. It’s a great alternative to traditional email providers.

3. **DuckDuckGo** – If you want to avoid being tracked online, DuckDuckGo is the search engine for you. It doesn’t store your data or track your activity.

4. **Tor** – Tor is the ultimate tool for anonymity. It routes your traffic through multiple nodes to hide your location and activity.

5. **Ghostery** – Ghostery helps you block trackers and ads on websites. It’s a must-have for anyone who values online privacy.

6. **Malwarebytes** – Malwarebytes is an essential tool for protecting your devices from malware and other threats.

7. **Bitwarden** – Bitwarden is a free password manager that keeps your passwords secure. It uses end-to-end encryption and is easy to use.

8. **VeraCrypt** – For securing your files, VeraCrypt is one of the best tools available. It offers military-grade encryption and is open source.

9. **uBlock Origin** – uBlock Origin blocks ads and trackers on your browser. It’s a lightweight and effective tool for privacy.

10. **Firefox Private Browsing** – Firefox’s private browsing mode is a simple yet effective way to protect your online activity.
