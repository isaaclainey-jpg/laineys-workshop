## Pro Tips for Maximizing Your VPS ROI

Here’s how to squeeze every last drop of value from your VPS:

- **Use containers (Docker)** – Run multiple apps on one server without conflicts. Saves RAM and makes backups easy.
- **Enable automatic backups** – Most providers offer this. It costs a little extra, but it’s worth it if you lose data.
- **Monitor your usage** – Use tools like `htop`, `iotop`, or even a free Grafana dashboard. Spot bottlenecks early.
- **Use a reverse proxy (like Nginx)** – Host multiple apps on one IP. Saves money and simplifies access.
- **Upgrade only when needed** – Don’t jump to a bigger plan just because you *can*. Monitor your CPU and RAM usage first.

And here’s a pro move: **use a CDN (like Cloudflare) for static content**. It reduces your server load and speeds up access for users worldwide—without costing more.
