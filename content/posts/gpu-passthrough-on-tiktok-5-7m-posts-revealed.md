## What is GPU Passthrough and How Does It Work

GPU Passthrough is a technique that lets a virtual machine or a container access a physical GPU directly. This means the GPU can be used by a single application without interference from the operating system. It's commonly used in gaming and 3D rendering to maximize performance.

On TikTok, creators often explain how this works using simple analogies. For example, one video compared GPU Passthrough to a direct flight from one city to another, skipping layovers. This helps make the concept more relatable to a broader audience.

The process involves configuring the system's BIOS, installing the right drivers, and setting up virtualization software. It's not as complicated as it sounds, and many TikTok guides break it down step by step.
