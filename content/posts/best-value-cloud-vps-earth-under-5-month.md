## Hidden Costs to Avoid When Choosing a Cheap Cloud VPS

Here’s where most people get burned: the hidden fees.

- **Bandwidth overages** – Some providers charge extra after 1TB/month. That’s not rare. 
- **IP addresses** – Some charge $1–$2 per extra IP. If you’re running multiple sites, this adds up fast. 
- **Backups** – Often not included. If you need them, it’s another $1–$3/month. 
- **Support** – Free plans usually mean no live support. You’re on your own. 
- **Data transfer pricing** – Some charge per GB, even if you’re just hosting a blog.

Always read the fine print. Look for providers that include bandwidth, IPs, and backups in the base price. That’s how you avoid surprise bills.

## Bonus: Tips to Maximize Your VPS Budget

Here are 5 real tips we’ve used to stretch every dollar:

1. **Use lightweight Linux distros** – Stick with Alpine Linux or Debian Minimal. They use less RAM and boot faster. 
2. **Automate with scripts** – Set up one-click backups, monitoring, and updates using Bash or Ansible. 
3. **Enable swap space** – Even with 2GB RAM, adding 1GB swap helps during spikes. 
4. **Use a CDN** – Offload static files (images, CSS, JS) to Cloudflare or BunnyCDN. Saves bandwidth and speeds up your site. 
5. **Monitor usage** – Use tools like Netdata or UptimeRobot to catch issues early. Don’t wait for your site to crash.

Bonus: If you’re running a blog, use Nginx instead of Apache. It uses less memory and handles more requests per second.
