## Key Features to Look for in a Modern VPN

When choosing a modern VPN, there are a few key features you should look for. First, strong encryption is essential. It's what keeps your data safe from prying eyes. Second, a no-logs policy ensures that your online activity isn't being recorded or sold.

Another important feature is a kill switch. This automatically disconnects your internet if the VPN connection drops, preventing any data from being exposed. Also, having a wide range of server locations can help you bypass geo-restrictions and access content from around the world.
