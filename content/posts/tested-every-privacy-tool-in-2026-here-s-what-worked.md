## How to Choose the Right Privacy Tool for You

You don’t need every tool in the world. What you need is a *smart stack*.

Start by asking: *What am I protecting?* 
- If it’s your online identity → use a good VPN + encrypted browser. 
- If it’s your emails → go with Tutanota or ProtonMail. 
- If it’s your passwords → Bitwarden or KeePassXC. 

Then check for three things: 
1. **Open-source code** (you can verify it). 
2. **No data logging** (look for audits or zero-log policies). 
3. **Independent verification** (like the ones from Cure53 or Mozilla’s Privacy Team). 

Avoid tools that ask for your phone number, email, or payment info upfront. That’s a red flag. If they want your info before you even use the tool, they’re probably selling it.

And don’t fall for “free” unless it’s truly free—no ads, no data collection. Most “free” tools in 2026 are just data farms in disguise.
